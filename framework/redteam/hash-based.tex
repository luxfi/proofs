\noindent Review a hash-based scheme as a stateful/stateless one-time-key
protocol, NOT a lattice one --- the Module-LWE concepts ($c\!\cdot\!s_2$,
$r_0$, LowBits, BCC) DO NOT apply. The central invariant:

\begin{quote}\itshape No SLH-DSA one-time signing material (FORS/WOTS+ secret,
SK.seed, SK.prf, chain start, unrevealed auth path) is ever reconstructed,
reused, or revealed outside the exact public signature elements a FIPS 205
verifier requires.\end{quote}

\section{Impossibility boundary (read first)}
Cheap, oracle-respecting thresholdization of an extractable hash-based scheme
is IMPOSSIBLE in the no-dealer/no-preprocessing model a public, leaderless,
permissionless chain needs (Kondi--Kumar--Vanegas). So the only safe
production options are: (a) threshold CERTIFICATION over INDEPENDENT FIPS 205
signatures (a STARK/FRI quorum proof), or (b) generic active-secure MPC that
evaluates SHAKE/SHA-2 INSIDE the computation (T-SLH-DSA-MPC: research-grade,
expensive). A scheme that ``shares the seed and reconstructs at a combiner'' is
NOT threshold signing --- flag it as such.

\section{One-time-key safety}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item Does any party or aggregator ever learn a complete FORS secret element
    or a complete WOTS+ chain secret?
  \item Can the same SLH-DSA leaf index be used twice (across signers, attempts,
    a Byzantine aggregator replaying a request, crash recovery, or snapshot
    rollback)? Is the per-leaf reservation atomic and single-use?
  \item Is the index/domain separation collision-resistant across the committee?
\end{enumerate}

\section{Address / domain separation (FIPS 205 ADRS)}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item Are layer, tree, key-pair, chain, hash, and FORS-tree ADRS fields all
    bound exactly per FIPS 205?
  \item Is the message digest / FORS-index derivation byte-compatible with the
    standard verifier? Is \texttt{opt\_rand} / randomized hashing handled
    safely in threshold mode?
\end{enumerate}

\section{Seed / key-share non-reconstruction}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item Is SK.seed / SK.prf ever reconstructed in one buffer at a combiner?
    (``strict-atom'' that only renames the buffer does NOT change the bytes ---
    see \texttt{framework/checks/proof-by-rename.sh}.)
  \item Does the DKG ever publish a party's secret contribution (open-reveal),
    making the master reconstructible by any transcript observer? The
    production path must use complaint-conditional reveal or fail closed.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Output and accountability}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item Does the final signature verify byte-for-byte under an UNMODIFIED,
    INDEPENDENT FIPS 205 verifier (e.g. circl AND pq-crystals)?
  \item Is the signer bitmap + transcript root bound to the final signature
    (no accountability forgery)? Are debug seed exports build-tag-gated out of
    production?
\end{enumerate}
